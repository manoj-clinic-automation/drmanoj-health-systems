“Add to Family Kitchen” — the Share button

With this shortcut, a recipe that arrives on WhatsApp, a web page, a photo of a handwritten recipe or a PDF goes to your Recipe Inbox with two taps: Share → Add to Family Kitchen. Nothing enters the Family Kitchen until you (or your caretaker) check it in the Inbox and press Confirm.

If someone sent you the shortcut link (most people)

  1. Open the link on your iPhone and tap Add Shortcut.
  2. It asks two questions. The answers are in your diary: Family Kitchen → Share shortcut. Copy the Address into the first and the Key into the second.
  3. Done. Try it: open any recipe in WhatsApp, tap Share (or hold the message → Forward → Share), then Add to Family Kitchen.

Your key is yours: it can only add to your own Inbox. If it is ever shared by mistake, your caretaker can issue a new one.

If you use only the recipe book (a kitchen member)

  1. Open your Family Kitchen link (it looks like family.dr-manoj.in/kitchen/k…/), sign in with your PIN and tap Me.
  2. Under Share shortcut you will find your own Address and Key. Paste them into the two questions the shortcut asks.
  3. Shared recipes land in your Inbox. Check what was read, correct anything marked, and tap Publish. It then appears for the whole family as “Recipe by <your name>”. If the same dish is already there, you can publish yours as your own version, kept side by side.

You can also paste a recipe or a link, or choose a photo or a PDF, under Add — no shortcut needed.

Building it once (the caretaker, about 5 minutes)

  1. Open Shortcuts → +. Name it Add to Family Kitchen.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (details) → switch on Show in Share Sheet. Under Share Sheet Types keep Images, PDFs, Text, URLs, Safari web pages, Files.
  3. The first block now reads “Receive Images, PDFs, Text… from Share Sheet”. Set If there's no input to Ask For → Text (so it also works when opened on its own: type or paste).
  4. Add the action Text. Type the address (from your Family Kitchen → Share shortcut). Add another Text action with the key.
  5. Add Get Contents of URL. URL: the first Text. Tap Show More:
    • Method: POST
    • Headers: add Authorization with the value Bearer  followed by the second Text (the key)
    • Request Body: Form. Add a field file, type File, value Shortcut Input. Add a field text, type Text, value Shortcut Input.
  6. Add Show Notification: “Saved to your Recipe Inbox”.
  7. To share it with the family: ⓘ → Setup (or “Import Questions”) → add a question for the address Text and one for the key Text, so each person answers with their own. Then Share → Copy iCloud Link and send the link.

Instagram and Facebook do not let readers in: the link is kept in the Inbox, and it asks for a screenshot — share the screenshot the same way. Photos of handwritten recipes, in Hindi or English, are read by the same reader that files your reports.

What happens next

The Inbox shows what was read, with the original photo or link beside it, and marks anything uncertain: a word that could not be read, an ingredient with no amount, an ingredient not in the food table. Correct it, tap Confirm (or Publish), and it joins the Family Kitchen for everyone, credited to you. If the same recipe is already there, it says so first. Nobody approves it — and only you can edit or unpublish what you added.
